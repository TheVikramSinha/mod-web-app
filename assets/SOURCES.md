## Menu category artwork — 7 October 2026

`menu-*.svg` are locally authored MOD-color category illustrations for items without supplied photography. They are placeholders, not photographs of the listed products. Catalog names and visible values were transcribed from the menu screenshot supplied in chat; incomplete values remain illustrative as documented in the menu/loyalty comparison README.
